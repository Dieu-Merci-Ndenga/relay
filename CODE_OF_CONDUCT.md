# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in Relay.

- Be kind, constructive and professional; assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
